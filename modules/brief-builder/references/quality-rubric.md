# Brief Quality Rubric

Score each criterion 0, 1, or 2. Maximum score is 20.

- **0:** missing or too vague to act on
- **1:** present but partial, or measurable in theory only
- **2:** clear, specific, and actionable

| # | Criterion | What a 2 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Objective clarity | One primary business objective, stated as an outcome and not a tactic |
| 2 | Measurable success | Metric, baseline, target, timeframe, and measurement source all given |
| 3 | Audience specificity | A defined group described by behavior, not "everyone" |
| 4 | Insight or tension | A human truth or tension the work can use |
| 5 | Single-minded proposition | One sentence takeaway with supporting reasons to believe |
| 6 | Deliverables and channels | Formats, quantities, sizes, channels, and languages listed |
| 7 | Budget | A figure or range, with what it covers |
| 8 | Timeline | Dates with approval stages built in |
| 9 | Mandatories and constraints | Legal, brand, and category requirements named, with owners |
| 10 | Ownership | A named decision-maker, approvers, and number of review rounds |

## Readiness bands

| Score | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 17 to 20 | Ready |
| 12 to 16 | Ready with assumptions (list them) |
| 0 to 11 | Not ready |

## Automatic blockers

A brief is **Not ready** regardless of score if any of these is missing:
- a primary objective
- a deadline
- the deliverables
- a named final approver
