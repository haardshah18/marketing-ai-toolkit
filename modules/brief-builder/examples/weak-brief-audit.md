# Example: auditing a weak brief

All names are fictional.

## The brief as received

> Subject: Social campaign
>
> Hi team, we're launching our new cold-brew tea range next month and need a fun social campaign. Make it viral, young and fresh. Something like what [Competitor] did last summer. Budget is flexible. Let me know when you can share ideas.

## What an audit looks like

**Score: 3 / 20. Verdict: Not ready.**

| # | Criterion | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Objective clarity | 0 | No objective stated; "social campaign" is a tactic |
| 2 | Measurable success | 0 | "Viral" has no metric |
| 3 | Audience specificity | 1 | "Young" only |
| 4 | Insight or tension | 0 | None |
| 5 | Single-minded proposition | 0 | None |
| 6 | Deliverables and channels | 1 | "Social" only; no formats or quantities |
| 7 | Budget | 0 | "Flexible" is not a budget |
| 8 | Timeline | 1 | "Next month" for launch, nothing for approvals |
| 9 | Mandatories and constraints | 0 | None |
| 10 | Ownership | 0 | No approver named |

**Blockers:** primary objective, named approver, concrete deliverables.

**Questions to send back (most important first):**
1. What should this campaign achieve in numbers (for example first-time orders, trial, or sales) and by when?
2. Who exactly are we trying to reach, and what do they drink today instead?
3. Who signs off on the work, and how many review rounds do we have before launch?
4. Which platforms and how many pieces are expected?

**Weakest lines, rewritten:**
- "Make it viral" becomes "Reach 500,000 people in our target audience and drive 4,000 first-time orders within eight weeks of launch."
- "Something like what [Competitor] did" becomes "Competitor work we admire, with what specifically we like about each (tone, format, idea)."
- "Budget is flexible" becomes "Production budget of [range], media budget of [range], to be confirmed by [name] by [date]."

See `strong-brief.md` for how this brief looks once the gaps are closed.
