# Example: a complete brief

Fictional brand and numbers, for illustration only.

## 1. Project snapshot
- **Project name:** Brightleaf Cold Brew Launch
- **Requested by:** Maya Ortiz, Head of Marketing
- **Brief owner:** Sam Lee, Account Director
- **Date and version:** v1.0
- **Final approver:** Maya Ortiz

## 2. Business context and problem
Brightleaf Tea Co. sells loose-leaf tea online. It is launching a ready-to-drink cold-brew range to reach new customers beyond existing tea drinkers. Online new-customer acquisition has plateaued at about 1,100 per month.

## 3. Objective
- **Primary objective:** Win first-time online customers with the cold-brew launch.
- **Success metric:** First-time orders containing cold-brew.
- **Baseline today:** 1,100 new customers per month (all products).
- **Target:** 4,000 first-time cold-brew orders.
- **Timeframe:** Eight weeks from launch.
- **How it will be measured:** Store order data with tagged campaign links, reviewed weekly.
- **Secondary objectives:** Grow email list by 5,000 sign-ups.

## 4. Audience
- **Who they are:** City professionals aged 24 to 34 who buy bottled iced tea from convenience stores at least weekly.
- **What they do today:** Reach for bottled tea in the afternoon and dislike how sweet it is.
- **Insight:** "I want something cold and refreshing, but every bottled tea tastes like sugar water."

## 5. Single-minded proposition
Cold-brewed and barely sweet: tea that actually tastes like tea.
- **Reasons to believe:** Brewed cold for 12 hours; 4 g sugar per 330 ml bottle (source: product lab sheet, to be attached by Maya before production).

## 6. Tone and guardrails
- **Tone:** Dry, confident, a little cheeky. Never preachy about health.
- **Brand rules:** Brand guidelines v3; logo lockup on all pieces.
- **Mandatories:** Sugar claims must match the lab sheet exactly.
- **Avoid:** Health or weight-loss claims; comparisons naming competitors.

## 7. Deliverables and channels
| Deliverable | Format and size | Quantity | Channel | Language |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hero film | 30 s and 15 s cut, 9:16 and 16:9 | 1 film, 2 cuts | Social and web | English |
| Static posts | 1080 x 1350 | 6 | Social | English |
| Short videos | 9:16, up to 20 s | 4 | Social | English |
| Launch email | Responsive HTML | 1 | Email | English |

## 8. Budget, timeline, approvals
- **Budget:** Production USD 25,000 to 30,000; media budget managed separately by Maya.
- **Milestones:** Concept presentation week 2; production start week 4; final delivery week 7; launch week 9 (all counted from brief sign-off).
- **Approvals:** Concepts and final work signed off by Maya; claims reviewed by Legal before production and before launch.
- **Review rounds:** Two per stage.

## 9. Constraints, risks, references
- **Constraints:** Filming limited to two days; bottle design is fixed.
- **Risks:** Sugar-content claims; launch date depends on bottling schedule.
- **Learned before:** Last summer's "healthy hydration" angle underperformed with this audience.
- **References:** Dry-humor beverage campaigns we like for tone only; no visual imitation.
